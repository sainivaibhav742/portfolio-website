# Portfolio Design Research — Socialmoon

## Research signal

The review of portfolio references supports a focused approach: strong identity and concise work framing are more persuasive than visual spectacle alone. The redesign will use a meaningful hero, distinct project evidence, restrained interaction, and clear paths to contact instead of copying any reference pattern.

| Finding | Implication for Socialmoon |
| --- | --- |
| Strong portfolios frame selected work as a narrative, not a thumbnail archive. [Figma, 2026](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/portfolio-website-examples/) | Place Socialmoon and BPOlytix as active operating work, then use a small, explicitly labelled set of experiments for technical proof. |
| Clear role, problem, approach, and impact make case studies credible. [Figma, 2026](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/portfolio-website-examples/) | Use role and operating focus metadata on every work item; avoid inflating future or research-stage products. |
| Award-recognized portfolio work commonly uses a single coherent visual language and content-led presentation. [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/portfolio/) | Build one original visual system around Socialmoon’s orbit/field-note metaphor instead of combining trendy effects. |
| The strongest contemporary portfolios blend motion, storytelling, and bold aesthetics while retaining a distinctive voice. [Muzli, 2026](https://muz.li/blog/top-100-most-creative-and-unique-portfolio-websites-of-2025/) | Use a deliberately small amount of purposeful motion and custom visual assets; retain fast, direct navigation and accessible reading. |

## Design decisions carried forward

- The site will foreground Vaibhav Saini’s identity as a founder, CTO, developer, and AI engineer.
- Socialmoon and BPOlytix will be treated as company anchors, while queued work is labelled honestly by its stage.
- The experience will favour editorial sequence, evidence, and direct contact paths over generic skill chips, faux dashboards, testimonials, or repeated rounded cards.
- Visuals will be original assets created for this site and used in distinct contexts; they will not be recycled as generic background decoration.
